## The Content Ecosystem

Whether you’re here for live streams, hardware testing, or long-form critiques, this site is the ultimate archive for all my projects:

* **Is It Playable?** — A channel focused on testing game performance across emulation and compatibility layers (like Proton) on a high-end custom PC. Emulated games are judged on audio stutters, shader compilation, and lighting/texture bugs, while compatibility layers are evaluated on graphical stability and required Protontricks tweaks where necessary.

* **The Gaming Archive** — A preservation project showcasing what gaming has to offer from indie gems to old classics, spanning a variety of genres and a multitude of systems.

* **Twitch & Live Content** — Where I host live streams, comprehensive playthroughs and a slightly better than average skillset under the TRW handle.

* **Written Reviews** — In-depth critiques reviews of individual games on my quest to conquer the backlog.
