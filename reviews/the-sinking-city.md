A flooded Lovecraftian city where fish-like refugees and ape-like aristocracy coexist under an unexplained deluge. A private investigator arrives to investigate shared visions of the drowning city. No waypoints, no objective markers, no handholding. The investigation belongs to the detective alone.

Mind Palace evidence linking connects scattered findings into coherent deductions. Retrocognition allows viewing past events at specific locations, adding temporal depth. Archive research demands entering precise criteria rather than browsing results. Every address must be located manually through street names and district navigation.

A sprawling open world unfolds across multiple Oakmont districts, navigable by boat with dynamic time-of-day cycles. Fish-like Insmouth refugees and ape-like Throgmorton aristocracy populate the streets, each lending visual distinction. Side cases extend the main thread with further deduction. Boat travel paces exploration between districts.

The Mind Palace demands connecting evidence through logical association rather than following pre-determined paths. Combat encounters involve firearms and a shovel against hostile creatures, functional yet basic compared to the investigation systems. Crouching reduces detection, adding a stealth layer that complements the survival horror framing.

Infested areas respawn enemies after clearing, rendering combat progress temporary. Film grain and chromatic aberration cannot be toggled off, imposing a visual filter that clashes with the atmospheric art direction. DLC pricing at eleven pounds per expansion further dampens the post-game offering.

Three distinct endings branch from final decisions, each carrying a dark tone aligned with Lovecraftian themes. A skill tree offers detective, combat, and survival upgrades. Oakmont's flooded architecture and atmospheric lighting create a convincingly oppressive environment. The presentation reinforces a city consumed by unseen forces.

Overall, Mind Palace evidence linking, retrocognition, and archive research deliver a detective experience demanding analytical effort within a Lovecraftian open world. Basic combat and respawning enemies prevent survival horror elements from matching the investigative depth.
