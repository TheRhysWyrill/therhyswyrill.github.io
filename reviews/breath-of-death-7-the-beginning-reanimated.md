Breath of Death VII: The Beginning: Reanimated delivers a charming retro aesthetic, featuring a delightful 16-bit Super Nintendo-style presentation that serves as a significant visual upgrade over the original NES-style graphics. This vibrant look is paired with humorous writing and meta-commentary, filling the narrative with clever jokes, fourth-wall breaking moments, and fun references to classic gaming franchises like Castlevania, Resident Evil, and Diablo. Progression feels consistently rewarding thanks to an accessible level-up system that provides a meaningful choice between two distinct bonuses every time you gain a level.

The turn-based combat is enhanced by strategic mechanics, utilizing a combo system where you build up hits to unleash powerful finishers for a deeper layer of engagement. This system is further expanded by innovative Unite techniques that require multiple party members to cooperate to take down tougher enemies and bosses. To keep the pacing swift, the game includes generous health recovery that restores the party to full health after every battle alongside streamlined random encounters that can be completely exhausted to respect your time.

However, the comedic role-playing adventure still faces a few notable hurdles stemming from its balancing and navigation design. The journey suffers from inconsistent difficulty spikes, particularly during the early hours when you only have two party members and enemy combinations can quickly feel overwhelming. Finding critical items and hidden collectibles can also devolve into tedious pixel hunting for secrets because the visual indicators on the retro maps are occasionally too subtle to notice.

Combat clarity can be slightly hindered by unclear status effect mechanics, as it is not always obvious which specific enemy attacks cause conditions like stun or poison. Exploration faces its own issues with repetitive dungeon layouts, where areas like the sewers and laboratories feature looping paths and similar-looking rooms that make navigation confusing. Finally, the experience is held back by limited save slots for managing long-term progress and an abrupt soft-lock potential where missing specific gear early on can make later areas so difficult that you are forced to grind to compensate.

Overall Breath of Death VII: The Beginning is a remarkably charming, witty, and mechanically creative retro role-playing game that excels through its humorous meta-commentary, strategic combo-based combat, and player-friendly encounter design. While the brief adventure is occasionally dragged down by early difficulty spikes, repetitive dungeon layouts, vague status indicators, and subtle secret placement, the delightful 16-bit presentation and engaging progression systems ensure it delivers a highly entertaining parody for fans of classic turn-based games.
