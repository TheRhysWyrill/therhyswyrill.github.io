John awakens alone in a stasis pod aboard the Groom Lake, a massive corporate research ship orbiting Titan. Surrounded by corpses and stripped of memory, the investigation unfolds through environmental storytelling as staff logs reveal a corporation experimenting with cloning and human specimens. The isometric perspective provides a unique vantage point for navigating the ship's interconnected levels.

Point-and-click mechanics drive exploration through object examination, item combination, and puzzle solving. The inventory system uses drag-and-drop interface, while a hub-and-spoke tram system connects different sections of the ship. Voice acting adds immersion to the environmental narrative, with extensive logs from staff members building genuine dread throughout the experience.

The cloning facility backstory proves particularly compelling, with Jeremiah and Sullivan logs constructing a layered corporate horror narrative. Environmental details reward thorough exploration, from corporate memos to cloning reports scattered across terminals.

The stasis pod dosage puzzle stumbles with unclear PDA documentation, requiring cross-reference between three notes and physical diagrams without specifying target body parts. Some puzzles demand cryptic lateral thinking that interrupts the otherwise intuitive progression.

A game crash near the final ten minutes creates frustration compounded by the absence of auto-save functionality. Settings menus launch separately from the main game, requiring external configuration rather than in-game access.

Staff diary entries and environmental storytelling create engagement that persists despite technical friction. Length exceeds typical indie point-and-click expectations, providing substantial content across the investigation.

Overall, the environmental storytelling and corporate horror setting deliver a compelling point-and-click experience. Cryptic puzzles and technical instability prevent the atmosphere from fully establishing itself, creating a divisive ending to an otherwise engaging investigation.
