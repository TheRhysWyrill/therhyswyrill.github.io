Trees Hate You opens with a deliberately pathetic avatar assembled from a creator and abandoned in woodland where every trunk harbours intent. Trouble starts with a coin grab that ends back at the start, shoved by growth. From there the design shows its hand: readable silhouettes, brazen traps and constant escalation across forest, beach, snow and a celestial stretch. Absurdist tone keeps repeated collapse comic rather than cruel. Music swells with progress and sags on collapse, while tallies turn carnage into scoreboard. The result reads as a prank played with confidence, where being out-thought matters more than execution, marred only by a finale built on chance.

Trick construction forms the spine, teaching a rule clearly before punishing trust in it. Early rooms establish that shape predicts danger, then later rooms exploit that confidence with delayed falls, pursuing trunks and long-range fire. Adaptation sharpens the joke further, as repeated routes sprout fresh hazards that discourage reliance on memory. Checkpoints at the mouth of each level or tunnel keep momentum intact. The cumulative effect rewards suspicion over speed, with each decoded room paying off briefly before the next inversion.

Atmosphere builds through place and sound rather than story, since narrative never extends beyond hostile woodland and a creator-built victim. A coconut beach introduces arcing bombardment from palms, a winter field hides trunks inside snowman costumes, and a celestial stretch pushes verticality to a peak. Between trials, an arcade cabinet offers a game within the game, and a quiet fishing spot invites pause. Soundtrack brightens with escalation, then slows audibly on collapse in a neat comment on failure. Death tallies, frequent jokes and generous respawn placement keep the mood slapstick as hazards multiply.

Flat-topped gun-trees anchor a visual grammar where silhouette predicts behaviour with unusual clarity. Spiky forms flatten on contact, round forms shove hard enough to reset progress, and coconut palms lob ordnance in arcs that demand timing over pace. Costume snowmen extend the vocabulary into winter, concealing familiar trunk aggression beneath festive padding. A tree gauntlet tests the full lexicon in sequence, granting hats such as the dunce cap before snatching the prize away. The hardest legitimate exam pairs two spiky forms with an RPG tree, solved only by launching skyward onto the checkpoint. That chamber shows the design at its best: legible, strict and solvable through observation.

Coin collection and chest opening feed a compact skill tree that functions as an extended prank. Vertical launch grants sudden height for crossing gaps or reaching checkpoints from odd angles. Speed upgrades shorten ground coverage. A floating shield repositions toward danger rather than blocking it, demanding awareness of facing. The summonable sword proves actively hostile, cutting on summon and turning corridors into self-inflicted gauntlets. Discovery that skills can be switched off arrives late, reframing several earlier struggles as optional handicaps rather than fixed difficulty.

Signage bait forms the most persistent irritant, as placards urging close reading lead directly into traps and teach distrust of curiosity. Hat rewards compound the tease, granted for survival feats such as the tree gauntlet and then removed or lost before value accrues. Physics prop handling adds scrappiness at close quarters, where control feels slightly loose around shoves and carries. Rage framing sits uneasily against generous checkpoint placement and constant jokes, softening intended frustration into comic routine. Together these frictions cost momentum without breaking it, since each setback reads as part of the prank.

Final confrontation abandons the readable structure that sustains the rest of the design. Random spawns place gun-trees and coconut palms above and around entrances, making outcomes depend on draw rather than execution. Long-range fire combines with erratic arcs to seal exits before patterns emerge. The absence of agency stands out because checkpoints elsewhere preserve learning through repetition. As a closing exam the encounter misjudges the brief, substituting luck for the trick literacy built across every prior biome.

Late discovery of the skill toggle reframes the middle act without excusing weak signposting. Several chambers blocked by sword arcs or shield facing become straightforward once unwanted abilities are disabled, which highlights how poorly the option is surfaced. Generous checkpoint placement limits the cost of that discovery, holding losses to seconds rather than stages. Dynamic music and absurd staging absorb remaining sting, with slowing motifs and costume gags restoring humour after harsh rooms. The balance holds because fresh silhouettes, traps and places arrive until the final arena.

Overall, silhouette literacy, adaptive traps, a prank skill tree and constant biome turnover deliver a confident comic design undone only at the final hurdle by random spawns. Strong signposting gaps around the sword and the skill toggle, plus bait placards that punish curiosity, mark clear limits without souring the whole. The journey earns its recommendation through readable trickery, even as the last fight demonstrates how quickly chance dissolves carefully taught mastery.
