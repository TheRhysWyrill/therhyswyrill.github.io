Planet Laika establishes a deeply unsettling Martian colony where residents have surrendered their faces, grounding its narrative in a persistent sense of hallucinogenic dread. This surreal premise forces players to confront a world defined by shared identity crises and a landscape that actively reflects the crumbling psyche of its inhabitants.

Core progression involves shifting between distinct personalities by interacting with specific mirrors or items to overcome environmental obstacles. Managing personas like the destructive Earnest or the observant Spacer creates a bizarre, innovative loop that asks the participant to master the internal rules of the human mind to move forward.

Occasional reliance on obscure progression triggers frequently stalls the pace, transforming simple navigation into a confusing test of intuition. Players often struggle to decipher how specific objects influence active mental states, turning the pursuit of objectives into an exercise in searching for hidden, non-intuitive solutions across the alien terrain.

Combat encounters utilise a mind core mechanic that requires volleying pulses between the protagonist and foes in a rhythmic, albeit fundamentally awkward, exchange. These battles feel like disjointed interruptions to the investigation, lacking the tactile satisfaction needed to sustain interest whenever the planet chooses to manifest its immediate, hostile opposition.

Technical execution relies on frequent pre-rendered sequences that, while ambitious, create jarring transitions when jumping between intense cinematic storytelling and actual exploration. Mastering the idiosyncratic menu navigation and visual cues for swapping identities requires a steep learning curve that can feel cumbersome for those accustomed to modern interface standardisation.

Surreal imagery elevates the experience into an introspective journey that rejects standard tropes in favour of a genuinely strange, collective consciousness. Although the erratic combat and cryptic nature of the world demand significant patience, the commitment to such an avant-garde vision ensures the journey remains memorable throughout the entire session.

Overall, the personality-switching system transforms exploration into a fever-dream sequence that rewards those willing to overlook substantial technical and mechanical friction. Investigating the secrets of this fractured consciousness provides a unique, unsettling perspective on the genre, creating a challenging yet deeply creative odyssey for anyone valuing atmosphere over polish.
