1990s Indonesia sets the scene for this narrative adventure. High school sweethearts Atma and Raya navigate their final school days together. A space dive mechanic allows entry into people's hearts. Warm pixel art and nostalgic detail establish the atmosphere.

The narrative tackles depression and suicide with care, weaving these themes into the story's progression. Character relationships drive emotional investment, with Atma and Raya's banter providing depth. The writing balances warmth against heavier moments. Care taken with sensitive subject matter elevates the experience.

The space dive mechanic transforms exploration into psychological investigation, entering hearts to resolve inner turmoil. Each dive presents distinct visual themes reflecting the target's state. A bucket list progression drives activities from watching movies to collecting bottle caps. Cat-petting serves as a charming side activity.

Space diving demands navigating surreal internal landscapes, solving environmental puzzles to help characters confront their struggles. The mechanic evolves across the narrative, introducing new challenges and visual complexity. Each encounter deepens the connection between gameplay and emotional storytelling.

Exploration occasionally lacks direction, with the town's layout sometimes obscuring the next objective. The heavy themes of depression and suicide may prove triggering for some players, though the game provides content warnings. These elements represent minor friction against an otherwise compelling experience.

Mojiken Studio renders 1990s Indonesia with warm pixel art, from bustling markets to quiet streets. The nostalgic detail establishes a distinctive atmosphere that sustains emotional investment throughout. Visual storytelling complements the narrative themes effectively.

Overall, a heartfelt narrative adventure tackles mental health themes with care through a unique space dive mechanic and compelling character relationships. The 1990s Indonesian setting provides a distinctive atmosphere that sustains emotional investment.
