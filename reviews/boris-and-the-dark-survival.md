Boris the Wolf navigates a dark, procedurally generated studio where the Ink Demon stalks through corridors searching for prey. The top-down perspective provides a fresh angle on the Bendy universe, establishing tension through overhead visibility that reveals threats approaching from multiple directions. Audio logs from Mila add emotional weight to each exploration run.

Multiple stalkers diversify the threat landscape, with the Butcher Gang appearing intermittently alongside the persistent Ink Demon. Each chaser carries a unique audio signature, allowing identification by sound alone before visual contact occurs. This design rewards attentive listening and creates distinct encounters that demand different evasion strategies.

Collectibles scattered across procedurally generated levels include scrap, fuses, dance steps, songs, and candles that unlock gallery entries and a Pinball mini-game. Finding these items requires navigating corridors while managing limited hiding spots, as only specific hide boxes provide safety from pursuing stalkers. Dead ends compound this tension, cornering Boris with no escape route when stalkers close in.

The RNG nature of level generation creates inconsistent pacing, with some runs extending far beyond expectations due to unfavourable item placement. Bacon soup consumes during sprinting but does not replenish naturally, limiting escape options during desperate moments. These mechanics combine to create scarcity that reinforces the survival atmosphere.

The core gameplay loop remains basic throughout, repeating the same search-and-evade pattern across each session. Dead ends and limited hiding spots create genuine tension, though the lack of a conclusive ending leaves the experience feeling incomplete. Collectibles motivate repeated runs, but the fundamental mechanics do not evolve.

Overall, the audio design and multiple stalkers deliver a survival horror that captures the Bendy universe's tension, though the basic gameplay loop and procedural inconsistency prevent the experience from sustaining its momentum.
