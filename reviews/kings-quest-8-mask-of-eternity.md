Connor of Daventry, a humble farmer, must collect five fragments of the Mask of Eternity to restore order to a fractured land. The premise establishes a dark fantasy tone distinct from the series' fairy tale roots, with regions spanning medieval villages and fiery volcanic landscapes. The 3D action-adventure gameplay represents a radical departure from point-and-click foundations. For 1998, the ambition and scope prove genuinely impressive.

Third-person combat handles sword strikes, crossbow bolts, and axe throws against varied enemy types across the diverse regions. RPG elements include a level-up system, multiple weapons, and shops for upgrades. The Dimension of Death introduces atmospheric horror elements, while the Underground Realm of Gnomes provides lighter puzzle-focused exploration. Metroidvania-style progression unlocks previously inaccessible areas once new items are acquired.

Art direction presents detailed 3D environments that capture each region's distinct atmosphere. Voice acting delivers strong performances with accurate lip-syncing for the era. The interior music impresses with atmospheric compositions. Character models convey personality despite technical limitations.

Combat feels janky with no clear indication of hit or miss, creating frustration during extended encounters. Precision jumping demands exact timing with only two modes: a bunny hop or a full leap. Loading between areas proves painfully slow, even on modern hardware. Easy difficulty becomes practically necessary to avoid combat frustration.

The ambitious scope for 1998 creates a surprisingly engaging experience despite mechanical rough edges. Varied regions and RPG-style progression maintain exploration momentum throughout the journey. The dark fantasy atmosphere provides a compelling backdrop that elevates the action-adventure formula.

Overall, this surprisingly enjoyable action-adventure deserves recognition beyond its divisive reputation. Janky combat and imprecise jumping mechanics prevent it from fully realising its potential.
