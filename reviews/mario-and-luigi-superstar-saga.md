Princess Peach's voice is stolen by the villain Cackletta from the Bean Bean Kingdom, replaced with explosive expletives. The premise establishes a humorous, self-aware tone with snarky dialogue that undercuts every situation. Luigi's separate button inputs in battle and field create meaningful variety alongside Mario's actions. The writing establishes genuinely hilarious character dynamics from the opening moments.

Turn-based combat with real-time action commands requires precise timing for jumps and Bros attacks from both characters. Equipment and stat upgrades allow customisation across HP, BP, POW, Speed, and Stash. Bros attacks unlocked through progression provide satisfying strategic options during combat. The Bean Bean Kingdom rewards exploration with eccentric inhabitants and hidden secrets.

Art direction presents detailed pixel art with expressive character animations that capture the series' humour. The Bean Bean Kingdom's eccentric inhabitants create a memorable world filled with distinctive personalities. Sound design features memorable compositions shifting between whimsical exploration themes and dramatic boss arrangements. Fuffleful's increasingly unhinged speeches deliver genuinely entertaining dialogue highlights.

The final boss Cackletta possesses excessive health in the second phase, creating a gruelling encounter. The save system proves slow by modern standards, with lengthy save times disrupting momentum. Tutorial sections appear front-loaded though snarky dialogue effectively undercuts the tedium. Limited item drop rates compared to later remakes reduce accessibility of healing supplies.

The Bros attack system requiring precise timing from both characters simultaneously creates uniquely engaging combat. Luigi's solo section at Gafa Ruins demonstrates clever design that breaks up the established formula.

Overall, the brilliantly written RPG with satisfyingly precise combat proves some games stand the test of time. The slow save system and excessive final boss health occasionally undermine the experience.
