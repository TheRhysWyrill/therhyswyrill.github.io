Bulb Boy, a boy with a lightbulb for a head, must collect ingredients for a jar of cherries in this surreal, dream-like world. The premise establishes a darkly whimsical tone through environmental puzzles and brief cutscenes rather than dialogue, with the atmosphere mixing cute character designs with occasionally grotesque imagery.

The body-swapping mechanic provides the game's most distinctive feature, transitioning from Bulb Boy to a stretchy sausage creature to the family dog. Each form introduces entirely different gameplay: the sausage body chapter adds stealth elements with a pursuing creature while solving valve puzzles, and the dog chapter features a dual-character mechanic splitting attention between two halves of the screen.

The sausage body chapter excels through its stretch mechanic, allowing navigation past pursuing creatures while solving environmental puzzles. Combat encounters remain absent, with progression gated entirely by puzzle solutions and item collection. The dual-character mechanic in the dog chapter demands splitting attention between two simultaneous problem spaces.

The flea removal puzzle represents the game's most frustrating sequence, requiring a contaminated sandwich to be fed to a character who isn't the dog. Minimal hints and opaque logic mean solutions often feel arbitrary rather than earned. Some items prove difficult to interact with due to visual overlap, and the repeated sight gag of pronounced butt cracks becomes tiresome. Puzzle logic ranges from satisfying to opaque across the entire experience.

Overall, the body-swapping variety and improved colour visuals deliver a memorable point-and-click experience, though opaque puzzle logic and a few unintuitive sequences prevent the darkly whimsical atmosphere from fully establishing itself.
