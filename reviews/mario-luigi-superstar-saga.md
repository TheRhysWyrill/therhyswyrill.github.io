Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga proves that it still holds up incredibly well all these years later, delivering an adventure that remains a joy to experience from both a gameplay and visual perspective. The entire world is completely full of life and personality, backed by a witty script that still manages to prompt several genuine laugh out loud moments. This charm is perfectly captured in the unforgettable Professor E. Gadd scenes at the Starbeans Cafe, which remain absolute classics. The game also structures its world exploration beautifully, introducing new equipment and special field abilities that seamlessly unlock entirely new parts of the map. Along the way, players will encounter a decent variety of environmental puzzles, none of which ever become too difficult or frustrating to figure out.

The absolute pinnacle of the experience is the innovative combat system. Assigning each brother to a dedicated face button is a brilliant way to split up the action, giving players true agency by allowing them to time their inputs to actively prevent damage or unleash a powerful counterattack. This system possesses a great deal of tactical depth because every single enemy features a distinct visual tell indicating exactly which brother they are targeting. This engaging foundation is elevated by the fantastic Bros. Attacks, which reward mastery by unlocking advanced variations after repeated use. These advanced versions introduce completely different button prompts and can even be transformed into powerful, endless attacks if your reaction time is sharp enough.

However, the combat does suffer from one minor perspective issue during certain enemy encounters. Because of the isometric angle and visual presentation of the battle arena, some incoming enemy attacks directed toward Mario can be particularly difficult to judge in terms of depth perception. This can occasionally lead to frustrating mistimed jumps or missed counters simply because it is hard to tell exactly when the projectile or enemy will cross into his defensive line.

Overall Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga is a timeless and brilliantly crafted RPG that excels through its vibrant character writing, clever world progression, and exceptionally engaging turn-based combat system. While minor depth perception quirks during battle can occasionally test your patience, the sheer creativity of the advanced Bros. Attacks and the unforgettable comedic charm make it an absolute masterpiece that remains a definitive highlight of the franchise.
