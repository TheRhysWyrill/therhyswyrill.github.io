A post-apocalyptic Earth stretches across desolate wastelands, where a young girl restores life to barren landscapes by walking across surfaces. The premise unfolds without dialogue, relying on environmental storytelling through floating statues and scattered memories that reveal the world's destruction. From the opening sprint, the movement system establishes itself as the mechanical foundation, with multiple traversal options available immediately. Performance mode delivers smooth visual presentation across the stark, beautifully rendered environments.

Traversal mechanics layer sprinting, wall-riding, surfing on goop, and floating into a cohesive movement vocabulary that rewards exploration. Each oasis functions as a hub, with branching paths leading to vessels containing trapped life force that must be freed. The act of restoring life creates instant visual feedback, as barren grey landscapes transform into vibrant green ecosystems filled with trees and spirits. Environmental storytelling deepens through collectible memories scattered across each region.

The environmental restoration mechanic stands as the most distinctive element, with walking across surfaces triggering immediate transformation from wasteland to oasis. Visual spectacle accompanies each restoration, as barren terrain sprouts vegetation and releases spirits in real time. The mechanic sustains engagement across the journey, turning traversal itself into the primary reward loop.

Floaty controls create friction during precision platforming, where momentum-based movement clashes with narrow ledges and timed jumps. Technical instability manifests through crashes and occasional bugs, though some issues resolve themselves without intervention. Carrier bags emerge as surprisingly persistent obstacles, their physics-based movement creating unexpected challenge during traversal sequences.

Overall, After Us delivers a traversal-driven experience where environmental restoration and fluid movement mechanics create a uniquely satisfying exploration loop, anchored by visual spectacle and atmospheric environmental storytelling.
