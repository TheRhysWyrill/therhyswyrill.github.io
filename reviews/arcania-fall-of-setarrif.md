ArcaniA: Fall of Setarrif delivers excellent character progression, allowing you to import an existing character while maintaining your previous equipment and vital stats to provide a significant head start. This instant power trip is supported by generous XP rewards from enemies like demon wolves and mine crawlers, leading to rapid leveling alongside immediate access to high-end legendary gear like the Eye of Adanos and the venerable blade. The expansion remains engaging as a form of mindless fun, offering brief and refreshing gameplay sequences where the perspective shifts to established guest characters like Lester or Gorn.

The core gameplay loop thrives on versatile combat mechanics, making it highly satisfying to use lightning magic to guarantee stuns on blocking enemies or turn environmental traps against massive foes like trolls. Navigating the world showcases impressive environment design, captured beautifully through the shattered streets of the city of Setarrif and subtle visual details like a color-shifting loading orb. Crafting accessibility remains incredibly convenient since you maintain immediate access to all previously owned materials, allowing you to instantly brew powerful sublime potions right from the start.

However, this fantasy expansion still faces a few notable hurdles stemming from its brief duration and repetitive endgame encounters. The gameplay suffers from an incredibly short runtime relative to its retail price, leaving you frustrated by a brief campaign that feels like it should have been included in the base game. This pacing issue is compounded by tedious boss design, culminating in a final boss encounter that is a direct reskin of a previous enemy featuring repetitive, time-consuming invincibility phases and annoying waves of adds.

The user experience introduces additional friction through awkward inventory management, as the total lack of an encumbrance system leads to a cluttered menu overflowing with useless materials collected along the linear path. You will also encounter recurring movement restrictions that actively force your character to slow down to a tedious walk whenever you enter a building or interior space. This navigation annoyance is paired with inconsistent environmental hazards, where certain stage traps like lightning strikes feel completely non-threatening compared to the massive damage taken from fire.

The presentation is further marred by distracting visual bugs, including technical glitches where legendary armor pieces like Innos' Blessing fail to render correctly and cause character models to appear completely flat. The immersion experiences additional turbulence from dialogue and voice-over issues, introducing audio glitches where spoken lines fail to match the subtitles alongside a general lack of logic in character behaviors. Finally, technical audio issues occasionally muffle combat and environmental sound effects without any clear explanation.

Overall ArcaniA: Fall of Setarrif is a remarkably fun, fast-paced, and loot-heavy fantasy expansion that excels through its rapid character leveling, satisfying elemental stuns, and immediate access to powerful legendary gear. While the high-energy combat and enjoyable guest character segments ensure it stands as a comforting piece of arcade RPG action, the expansion is occasionally held back by uninspired boss reskins, flat-rendering armor glitches, cluttered inventories, and a steep price tag for its exceptionally brief length. Because of this incredibly short duration relative to its full retail cost, it is highly recommended to only pick up this title on a steep sale, otherwise it is best to skip it entirely.
