The US First Infantry Division, nicknamed the "Big Red One" for their distinctive arm patch, advances through the European theatre of World War II in this cinematic shooter. From the opening moments, the atmosphere derives from squad banter, period-accurate weapons, and historical documentary segments between chapters that ground the fictionalised gameplay in actual military history.

Level variety drives the experience through infantry combat, tank missions, plane gunnery sequences, and anti-aircraft gun sections. Each mission type introduces distinct mechanics, preventing the repetitive structure that plagues many WWII shooters.

The tank mission stands as a genuine highlight, delivering vehicular combat that breaks up the infantry formula with satisfying armour engagement sequences. Historical documentary segments between chapters provide educational context, revealing the real-world significance of the missions and the division's legacy. These interludes elevate the experience beyond a typical shooter by connecting the fictionalised gameplay to actual military history. Squad dialogue during these sequences adds personality and camaraderie.

Excessive grenade spam from enemies becomes increasingly frustrating in later levels, with hosts hurling projectiles at an unrealistic rate that overwhelms available cover. Screen shake during intense combat obscures visibility, making it difficult to track targets amid explosions. The leaning mechanic feels awkward due to D-pad mapping, limiting tactical options during critical firefights. Friendly fire rules remain inconsistent across missions.

Overall, the diverse mission design and historical documentary segments deliver a WWII shooter with real variety and substantial educational value, though grenade spam and excessive screen shake prevent the combat from reaching its full potential.
