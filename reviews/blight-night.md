Golden Springs, 1986. The entire population has risen from the dead, and one of the few survivors must navigate increasingly desperate situations across a small town overrun by mutants. The premise draws from 1980s horror cinema, establishing a dark aesthetic punctuated by radio broadcasts that fill the silence with local hits and narrative context.

Combat centres on stamina-based encounters against undead hordes, with weapons ranging from pistol to LMG requiring careful resource management. The stamina system drains rapidly during sustained combat, punishing aggressive play and demanding strategic retreats to recharge. Ragdoll physics add visceral satisfaction to each takedown, with the force of upgraded weapons launching enemies across the screen.

The LMG transforms combat once fully upgraded, delivering overwhelming firepower that trivialises most encounters. Sidearms like the shotgun prove difficult to justify given its limited application against clustered enemies. A weapon reload exploit allows bypassing reload animations entirely by unequipping and re-equipping through the infantry menu, breaking combat balance when discovered.

Section-based health regeneration restores vitality between encounters, with lamp posts serving as save points scattered across the map. The map system tracks explored areas with Silent Hill-style markers, guiding navigation through the town's winding streets. Digging up buried items and collecting vintage vinyl records add side activities that reward thorough exploration.

Radio broadcasts from WFOG 101.5 FM weave atmosphere throughout the journey, with DJ Jimmy Wilson providing both musical backdrop and world-building detail. The 1980s setting saturates every surface, from period-appropriate weapons to environmental references that reinforce the era's cultural identity. Walk speed, however, feels noticeably sluggish during backtracking segments, dragging exploration between combat zones.

Overall, the atmospheric radio system and weapon variety deliver a top-down survival horror with period charm, though stamina management and slow movement create friction that prevents the tension from sustaining itself across the full experience.
