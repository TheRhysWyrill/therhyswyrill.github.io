Near-future metropolises crumble under abnormal weather phenomena as special agent Shelia investigates a black hole threatening reality itself. The premise establishes a sci-fi conspiracy involving ancient soldiers and primordial floods, setting the stage for high-octane combat against relentless hostiles.

Combat demands split-second decisions between sword strikes, gunplay, and the grapple ability that pulls enemies into melee range. Sword and gun combos flow together with a hectic pace that rewards aggression, while bullet reflection stands among the game's most inventive systems, turning incoming fire into offensive opportunities with precise timing windows.

Blitz slash decimates health bars in two hits, providing a satisfying power fantasy against tougher adversaries. The skill tree unlocks upgrades for blade range, rocket punch, and ability cooldowns, though accessing the menu triggers occasional crashes that halt progression entirely. Grapple sequences punctuate combat encounters, pulling Shelia across gaps and into flanking positions.

The interconnected map design reveals a sprawling metropolis with verticality and multiple pathways through each zone. Environmental destruction crumbles concrete barriers and shatters glass panes, creating dynamic combat arenas that shift mid-fight. Stealth sections add variety between firefights, though the system lacks the depth of the core combat mechanics.

Limited weapon customisation restricts build experimentation, with most firearms feeling functionally identical despite cosmetic differences. Movement animations occasionally jitter during grapple transitions, breaking the otherwise fluid traversal between platforming sections and combat arenas.

Overall, the sword and gun hybrid combat delivers a visceral, fast-paced experience that rewards aggressive play, though skill tree crashes and shallow customisation prevent the action from reaching its full potential.
