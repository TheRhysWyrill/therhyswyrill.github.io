Hand-to-hand combat drives every encounter in this martial arts brawler where each room becomes a puzzle of crowd control and precise timing. Parrying enemy strikes builds structure damage, opening opponents to devastating finishers that chain across groups of five or more adversaries simultaneously. The satisfaction of clearing an entire warehouse floor without taking a hit rewards mastery in ways few beat 'em ups achieve.

Five distinct stages offer unique visual identities and enemy configurations, from the neon-lit nightclub with its thumping soundtrack to the serene museum galleries. Shortcuts unlock after completing stages without them, encouraging replays that reveal optimal routes through familiar spaces.

The age/death mechanic transforms failure from mere inconvenience into permanent consequence, with each death adding years to the protagonist's age until death at seventy ends the run. Skills unlock permanently through experience points, creating a roguelite meta-progression that makes each attempt feel purposeful.

Structure damage mirrors Sekiro's posture system, where sustained aggression breaks enemy defence faster than whittling health. Blocking absorbs incoming strikes but depletes a structure bar that, when full, staggers the attacker for a follow-up combination. This rhythm of parry, dodge, and counterattack creates a martial arts vocabulary that demands constant adaptation.

Boss encounters range from satisfying duels to gruelling health-gates, with the final confrontation stretching well beyond the difficulty established by preceding stages.

Five stages deliver distinct atmospheres and enemy types, with each location reinforcing the revenge premise through environmental storytelling and escalating challenge.

Overall, the combat mechanics and age system create a martial arts experience that rewards precision and punishes carelessness. The final boss's health-gating mechanics create friction that contrasts sharply with the otherwise fair difficulty curve, yet the core combat loop sustains momentum across multiple replays.
