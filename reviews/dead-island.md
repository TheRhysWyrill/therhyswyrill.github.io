A tropical resort island descends into zombie chaos, and four immune survivors must fight through beaches, jungles, and quarantine zones while searching for a cure. The first-person perspective drops directly into the action, with melee weapons replacing guns as the primary means of survival.

Melee combat drives the experience with weighty swings that feel physical and responsive. Each weapon type carries distinct speed and reach characteristics, while dismemberment removes limbs strategically rather than as spectacle. The drop-kick mechanic delivers crowd control with a satisfying impact, and the kick button provides emergency spacing when surrounded. Looting every bag, bin, and wallet along the way becomes compulsive, feeding a scavenging loop that sustains exploration momentum.

Character progression through the skill tree reinforces this loop, with combat perks and health regeneration making solo play viable. The workbench system upgrades salvaged weapons into increasingly effective tools, creating a satisfying power curve across the journey. Each of the four distinct areas introduces new threats and resource challenges, maintaining variety through escalating difficulty.

Driving sections, however, functional as they are, undermine the pace with tedious GPS navigation that interrupts the otherwise tight scavenging rhythm. The quarantine zone represents the most punishing stretch, dense with infected encounters and scarce resources that demand careful ammunition management. Weapon durability adds another friction layer, forcing constant repairs that drain both resources and momentum.

Overall, the weighty melee combat and physics-driven dismemberment deliver a visceral zombie action experience, and the tropical resort setting provides a refreshing backdrop for the survival loop. The looting and progression systems maintain steady engagement across the island's varied regions.
