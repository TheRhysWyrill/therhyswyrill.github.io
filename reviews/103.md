Looping corridors shift subtly with each pass in this first-person horror adventure. Unsettling dolls populate the spaces, and a soundtrack shifts from soothing to sinister. The atmosphere derives from environmental detail rather than jump scares. What makes this work is the absence of explicit threats.

Mirrors serve as portals to a shadow realm when stared at, creating visual tricks. Objects move between visits, whiteboards accumulate cryptic messages, and a teddy bear relocates unpredictably. The soundtrack morphs from gentle lullaby to threatening drone. Each detail reinforces the sense that the environment is never static.

A drinking mechanic progressively slows movement and warps the environment, combining physical disorientation with narrative revelation. The late-game sequence delivers emotional weight through escalating accusation. What begins as atmospheric horror transforms into something personally confronting. The drinking mechanic proves the most effective narrative device.

The mirror puzzle demands observing reflections in a specific order, though the correct sequence lacks clear guidance. Walking between two corridors that change on each pass forms the core loop, with noticing differences driving progression. Some textures appear smudged during close inspection.

Floaty movement and finicky controls lack a dedicated focus button, requiring camera alignment for interaction. The price-to-content ratio presents a significant barrier at full cost. At sale price, the atmospheric horror justifies the investment.

Static dolls enhance discomfort through their inability to move, creating moments of respite. The cozy art style with soft colours contrasts with sinister content. A figure appearing directly behind the player delivered the peak scare. The visual design sustains tension throughout.

Overall, atmospheric horror through looping corridors, unsettling dolls, and a shifting soundtrack delivers sustained unease. The price-to-content ratio and mirror puzzle confusion represent significant friction against the atmospheric achievement.
