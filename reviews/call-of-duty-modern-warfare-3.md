Task Force 141 continues their pursuit across New York, Hamburg, Berlin, and Siberia, with the stakes escalating as the antagonist's plans unfold. The atmosphere is consistently dark and urgent, maintaining tension while introducing new characters and mission types.

The Stronghold stealth mission stands as the campaign's tactical peak, with a fortress setting that demands careful movement and patience. The dark atmosphere and limited entry points create genuine tension, rewarding methodical play over aggressive engagement. This sequence provides welcome contrast to the louder action sequences.

Tank support during the Scorched Earth Berlin mission delivers a change of pace from typical infantry combat, with armoured vehicles providing covering fire through urban destruction. This variety prevents the campaign from becoming repetitive, with distinct combat scenarios maintaining momentum.

Chaotic firefights occasionally overwhelm with enemies appearing from unseen positions, creating frustrating deaths that feel unavoidable rather than challenging. The enemy placement sometimes prioritises spectacle over fairness, with bullet sponges and obscured spawn points that test patience rather than skill during intense urban combat.

The lack of controller support on PC creates an unexpected inconvenience, particularly for players accustomed to the franchise's standard input options. The keyboard and mouse controls remain functional but less comfortable, especially during vehicle sequences.

Environmental storytelling through destroyed cityscapes and desperate civilian evacuations reinforces the global scale of the conflict, grounding the scripted action in a world that feels genuinely imperilled by the narrative's events.

Overall, the Stronghold stealth mission and varied mission design establish a Modern Warfare trilogy conclusion with genuine emotional weight, though chaotic enemy placement and missing PC controller support prevent the experience from fully realising its potential.
