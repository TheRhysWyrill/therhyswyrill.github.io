American, British, and Soviet soldiers fight across multiple World War II campaigns, with each perspective offering distinct combat scenarios and squad dynamics. The atmosphere captures the era's intensity through historical mission settings that range from beach landings to urban warfare, establishing the franchise's signature tone.

The stealth mission aboard the Tirpitz provides a standout undercover experience that breaks the standard assault formula. The mechanical shift from aggressive combat to careful infiltration creates pacing diversity, rewarding patience and observation over reflexive engagement.

Multiple campaigns offer variety through different national perspectives, with each army bringing distinct equipment and mission types to the shared narrative. The variety prevents repetition across the campaign's structure, with the American, British, and Soviet missions each providing tonal differences that sustain engagement.

The absence of sprint creates a deliberate pacing that demands tactical positioning rather than reactive movement. This mechanical choice transforms firefights into methodical encounters where ammunition management and cover usage determine survival, though the limitation can feel restrictive during extended engagements.

Friendly fire causes instant game overs that create unforgiving difficulty spikes, particularly during intense sequences where squad members cluster near enemy positions. The mechanic demands careful aim but occasionally punishes the player for circumstances beyond their control.

The PC controls prove challenging for players accustomed to console shooters, with the lack of aim assist requiring precise mouse movements that test accuracy during chaotic firefights. The transition proves difficult but ultimately rewarding, with mastery creating satisfying combat encounters.

Overall, the multiple campaigns and stealth mission variety establish a classic WWII shooter that holds up despite dated mechanics, though the absence of sprint and awkward controls create friction that tests patience during extended play sessions.
