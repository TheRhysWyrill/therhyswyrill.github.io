Crow Country drops special agent Mara Forest into an abandoned theme park to investigate the disappearance of Edward Crow, opening with immediate Resident Evil parallels. Fixed camera angles and tank-style controls create that classic survival horror tension, though modern control options soften the learning curve. The fairy tale aesthetic blends whimsical elements with genuine horror, establishing a distinctive visual identity across themed areas.

Atmosphere drives the experience through haunting music and a disturbing theme park setting that escalates in strangeness. Environmental storytelling reveals a conspiracy involving gold mining and otherworldly visitors, rewarding careful exploration of every corner. The soundtrack pulses through corridors with unsettling compositions that match the on-screen tension.

Puzzle design demands multiple key items and environmental manipulation, creating satisfying progression through locked doors and hidden passages. Bronze keys, crank handles, ruby chains, and data discs gate access to new areas, maintaining momentum through increasingly complex puzzles. Weapon variety spans handgun, shotgun, magnum, and flamethrower, with ammunition management creating resource tension throughout combat encounters.

Camera positioning creates difficulty during aiming, particularly when the angle shifts away from directly behind the character. This limitation transforms straightforward encounters into tense manoeuvring exercises that test spatial awareness alongside shooting accuracy. The challenge becomes part of the survival horror identity rather than a mechanical failure.

Multiple themed areas, from Fairy Tale Town to Haunted Hilltop to Ocean Kingdom, provide environmental variety that sustains exploration. Each zone introduces distinct visual themes while maintaining the overarching fairy tale horror aesthetic. The progression system rewards thorough investigation with new weapons and key items that unlock previously inaccessible sections.

Overall, the haunting atmosphere and satisfying puzzle design deliver a Resident Evil homage that captures classic survival horror's essence. Camera positioning during combat creates occasional frustration, but the core mechanics and thematic presentation remain deeply satisfying throughout.
