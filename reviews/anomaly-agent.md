Agent 70 hunts four anomaly sources across a cyberpunk cityscape where time manipulation and corporate corruption collide, establishing immediate narrative stakes through a time loop that traps every character in repeated cycles. The premise unfolds through room-by-room encounters, with pop culture references from Kratos to Terminator saturating the world. From the opening moments, the soundtrack establishes an energetic tone that persists across every encounter. Pixel-art environments render distinct chapter aesthetics with visual personality.

Combat centres on fast-paced melee combos with a parry system that rewards precise timing, spawning an anomaly bat after successful parries that extends combo potential. Range combo finishers execute enemies with stylistic flair after two-hit sequences, creating satisfying punctuation to combat encounters. The dodge ability functions mid-combo without resetting the counter, maintaining offensive momentum during evasion. Room-by-room progression heals fully between encounters, creating accessible checkpoint pacing that encourages aggressive play.

The parry system stands as the most distinctive mechanical element, with the anomaly bat reward creating satisfying combat loops that sustain engagement across the campaign. Combining parries with range finishers and anomaly powers builds fluid combat chains that reward mastery. The generous checkpoint system ensures experimentation without punishing consequences, making challenging encounters approachable.

Shotgun enemies resist parrying entirely, creating encounters that demand evasion over the game's primary defensive mechanic. Their extended range compounds frustration, forcing retreat rather than the aggressive engagement the combat system encourages. The final boss encounter overwhelms with multiple enemy types and attack patterns simultaneously, creating chaos that disrupts the otherwise controlled combat rhythm.

Overall, Anomaly Agent delivers a stylish, accessible action experience anchored by a killer soundtrack and parry-driven combat that rewards aggressive play, despite some enemy design choices that clash with its core mechanics.
