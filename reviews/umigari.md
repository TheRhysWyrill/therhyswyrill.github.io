A whale-god curse traded humans and fish, leaving drowned cities where whispered talk sounds from flooded malls. School corridors lined with mannequin bullies and grotesque catch designs set an eerie, mournful register rather than constant terror. Harpoon-catch-upgrade repetition forms the spine, with bells, Torii gates and boat-speed gates marking progress to level five. Body designs carry the identity, from suited fish to uniformed kids, while Omega pursuits and dome dread supply intermittent shocks. Long calm stretches separate the scares, so atmosphere builds through eeriness more than assault.

Harpooning fish, loading the boat frame, selling to the fishmonger and buying speed, reel, sonar, boost and fuel drives the economy. Ringing bells repels Omega, turning sound into defence. District requests structure advancement through discrete fetch tasks. Bait-grinding funds deliveries where direct combat never appears, since fetch bosses replace fighting. Torii gates and boat-speed gates punctuate travel with thresholds and speed checks on the way to level five. Fuel management gates longer expeditions, since empty tanks strand loaded boats far from buyers.

Shrine tablets anchor the earliest fetch work, with three tablets gathered across shrine grounds. School corridors pair the accessories hunt with sonar acquisition. Mall aisles hide tires and an octopus hunt. Dome interiors combine bait-maker access with delivery chains. City districts stack oarfish, jellyfish, swordfish and piranha hauls alongside a train lever and a shark mortar fight.

Omega pursuits tear through calm water with chase pressure and sudden strikes. Dome dread builds through sustained eeriness and a single well-placed shock. Bells answer the pursuer directly, since ringing repels the hunter mid-chase. Sparse placement keeps horror intermittent, with long fishing and farming stretches between peaks. Jump scares land precisely because calm water dominates between encounters.

Suited fish swim in tailored menace, uniformed kids drift in formation and fingered tires grip with fleshy digits. Handed tuna, nosed hammerheads and a disgusting squid extend the grotesque across the roster. Mannequin bullies line school corridors with frozen cruelty. Whispered fish talk threads through flooded malls, keeping the curse audible. Valuable hauls carry person-like features, which keeps disgust close to every catch. School mannequin cruelty and mall flooding keep human traces visible inside the transformed geography.

Caught fish flop out of the boat when boosting or turning, repeatedly losing valuable flying fish, tuna and stingrays with no lock-in. Sonar pickup confusion stalls the octopus hunt, since the chest item never appears on the boat or interface. Swordfish cause the sharpest combat friction, punishing every approach. Late grind demands 150,000 to 300,000 for speed upgrades, best farmed on 6,000 flying fish, which renders late purchases pointless. Sparse horror stretches thin the dread between Omega chases, dome dread and jump scares. Janky boat-window harpooning compounds the losses whenever aim fights the frame at speed.

The whale-god curse frames everything, trading places between humans and fish toward promised peace. Drowned cities hold the aftermath in sunken streets and flooded interiors. Eerie, mournful tone persists through whispered talk and grotesque design rather than loud assault. District fetch bosses and bait-grinding sustain momentum where combat never enters. Weirdness stays the point throughout, strange and coherent in equal measure. Promised peace frames the curse as moral architecture rather than mere backdrop.

Overall, the harpoon-upgrade loop and grotesque catch designs carry a weird, mournful fishing expedition through drowned districts, Omega chases and fetch-driven progression. Lost catches, sonar confusion and steep late costs blur the finish, but the curse framework and eerie calm give the grind lasting character.
