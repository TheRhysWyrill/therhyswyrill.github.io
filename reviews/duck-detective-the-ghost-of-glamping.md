Duck Detective: The Ghost of Glamping delivers exceptional voice acting, acting as a major highlight that significantly enhances the avian cast and makes the entire experience feel vastly more polished than it would with text alone. This charming investigation thrives on clever mystery writing, showcasing an incredibly well-executed twist regarding the culprit’s true identity that provides a satisfying revelation for amateur sleuths. The underlying narrative hits all the right notes through nostalgic and witty humor, keeping the writing consistently funny by weaving relatable adult themes like bread addiction, overdue bills, and messy divorces into the feathered dialogue.

The core investigation loop remains highly engaging due to rewarding deduction mechanics, utilizing a fill-in-the-blanks system that offers a clear sense of progress as you piece the case together. Progressing through the glamping grounds relies on intuitive environmental storytelling, where critical clues like a subtle blonde strand of hair or a triple-L pattern are naturally integrated into the backgrounds to reward observant players. This loop benefits from meaningful quality-of-life improvements, including the ability to review evidence multiple times and a challenging Sleuth Mode that pairs beautifully with the strong character synergy between the cynical Duck Detective and his optimistic roommate Freddy.

However, this quirky detective adventure still faces a few notable hurdles stemming from its brief runtime and minor interface clunkiness. The gameplay suffers from abrupt narrative pacing that wraps up the entire case in under two hours, which can make the ultimate conclusion feel incredibly sudden just as the story's momentum is truly building. This swift resolution introduces a limited ghost payoff, meaning that the total lack of an actual supernatural element or a more substantial resolution to the haunting can feel slightly underwhelming compared to the previous game's mystery.

The user experience introduces additional friction through clunky menu navigation, as managing your growing suspect list and switching between separate deduction screens can occasionally feel a bit cumbersome when dealing with a larger cast. You will also encounter high-pressure quick-time decisions, forcing you to make permanent moral choices regarding who to arrest that can feel unnecessarily stressful when multiple characters are intertwined in the same crime. Finally, the mechanical variety can occasionally falter due to obscure item usage logic, showcasing a few arbitrary sequences like figuring out the exact combination required for the Live Laugh Love ritual.

Overall Duck Detective: The Ghost of Glamping is a remarkably witty, beautifully voiced, and intellectually satisfying detective adventure that excels through its clever deduction mechanics, hilarious adult humor, and charming environmental storytelling. While the cozy mystery is occasionally cut short by its brief two-hour runtime, a lackluster supernatural payoff, clunky suspect menus, and stressful endgame moral choices, the phenomenal character writing and rewarding quality-of-life updates ensure it stands as a delightful and highly entertaining case for fans of casual puzzle games.
