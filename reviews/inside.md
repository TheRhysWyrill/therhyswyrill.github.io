Inside is a super cool game that looks amazing and creates a really eerie, carefully built atmosphere. The puzzles are smart and just the right level of tricky. They are not too hard to make you rage but challenging enough to keep you thinking. The sound design is awesome too, with simple but powerful sounds like distant heartbeats that really crank up the tension and pull you in.

The story is pretty mysterious and leaves a lot up to you to figure out. Some people will love that, but others might find it a bit frustrating if they want clear answers. The ending comes out of nowhere and leaves you with more questions than answers, so you’ll probably be thinking about it for a while after you finish.

Overall, Inside is a beautiful and haunting game that mixes art, sound, and gameplay perfectly. The story might not be for everyone, but the whole experience is definitely worth checking out.
