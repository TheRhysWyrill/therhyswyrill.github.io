Writing objects and adjectives into existence forms the foundation of this DC Comics puzzle adventure, where Maxwell's notebook serves as the primary tool for environmental manipulation. The superhero licence expands the playfield across Gotham City and Planet OA, though word-creation remains central.

Each puzzle demands a different creative solution, with the notebook accepting any noun or adjective to generate objects. Combining descriptors with items produces unexpected results, such as equipping 'giant' and 'flaming' to create oversized weapons. The reputation system penalises overpowered solutions, pushing toward inventive approaches. This flexibility sustains engagement.

Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman, Green Lantern, and Flash feature in story missions across their respective territories. Origin stories provide context for each hero's background while offering puzzle sequences tailored to their abilities. The DC licence adds narrative variety that word-creation alone would not provide.

Randomly generated tasks lack the curated quality of Scribblenauts Unlimited, with many puzzles sharing similar solutions across locations. The repetition becomes apparent as the novelty of word-creation wears thin when applied to identical objectives. Progression gates behind reputation points earned from tasks.

Camera positioning proves problematic during combat sequences, where automatic adjustments obscure enemy positioning and make precise targeting difficult. Perspective shifts create confusion when facing multiple opponents, and the lack of manual camera control compounds the issue. Combat encounters become frustrating rather than challenging.

Visual presentation maintains a cartoon aesthetic that complements the DC licence, with character models representing their comic book counterparts. Music serves as background accompaniment without drawing attention, and the interface remains functional despite minor display quirks. The package delivers a competent puzzle experience.

Overall, the word-creation mechanic and DC superhero integration deliver a puzzle experience with creative potential, though randomly generated tasks and camera issues prevent it from matching its predecessor. The core concept remains compelling despite these limitations.
