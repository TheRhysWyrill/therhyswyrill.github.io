Baba Is You hands control of the rules themselves: word blocks sit on the grid alongside objects, and pushing them together rewrites the level's reality. "BABA IS YOU" makes the rabbit controllable; "WALL IS STOP" blocks movement; "FLAG IS WIN" sets the goal. Each puzzle begins as a familiar grid but demands lateral thinking to untangle the logic, turning programming concepts into play.

Over 200 levels span 15+ worlds, each introducing new mechanics that layer atop previous knowledge, with extra levels serving as harder elaborations of base puzzles. The "not" mechanic introduces double negatives that expand the logical possibilities, while community-made levels provide additional variations on the core systems.

Escalation drives the design: early puzzles teach through achievable challenges, then gradually compound rules until the grid becomes a web of interdependent conditions, with minimalist art and pleasant music fading into the background. Each solved puzzle delivers a rush of recognition that sustains momentum.

The "level is level" meta-mechanic transforms the overworld map itself into a playable puzzle, where the structure defining which levels exist becomes another set of rules to manipulate. This shift recontextualises the entire progression system, turning navigation into experimentation and rewarding those who probe the boundaries of each world.

The Depths cursor system becomes tedious: losing track of the cursor traps the player on a level with no clear path back. The "pull" mechanic proves consistently frustrating, as dragging objects backward through tight spaces creates awkward movement patterns.

Overall, the word-block rule manipulation delivers a puzzle experience whose logical depth persists long after the initial novelty fades, even if The Depths navigation and certain mechanics undermine the otherwise elegant design.
