Leaf-based melee combat and throwable seals define this compact Metroidvania set within a cursed shrine. Momo, a priestess from Koho village, ventures through interconnected corridors seeking the Underworld Queen. The free pay-what-you-want model on itch.io makes it an accessible entry point to the series.

Charm throw, dash, and double jump unlock new areas through classic Metroidvania progression gates. Health upgrades hidden as love letters reward thorough exploration across the shrine's interconnected rooms. New Game Plus and a developer room extend replayability beyond the initial playthrough.

Flying demons and heavily armoured enemies deal punishing damage, creating tension during corridor traversal. The green leaf upgrade arrives too late to meaningfully shift combat dynamics before the final encounters, while charm usage remains underutilised with options that feel optional rather than essential.

Prayer statues scattered throughout provide blessing opportunities that supplement health upgrades. The map system tracks explored areas but leaves completionists frustrated by obscure final collectibles.

NPC encounters and scattered love letters convey narrative through environmental storytelling rather than dialogue. The brief character interactions with Eerie and Melco add charm without disrupting exploration flow, while the story remains minimal as context for systemic progression. Pixel art uses muted tones and simple character sprites to establish the cursed shrine atmosphere.

The leaf as a weapon creates initial confusion, with its attack animation resembling a handbag rather than a clearly defined tool. Weapon identity clarifies gradually as charm combinations and upgrade paths become available.

Overall, the leaf-based combat and compact Metroidvania structure deliver satisfying exploration and progression in a wonderfully brief package. Punishing enemy damage and a late green leaf unlock create friction, but the tight controls and charming world sustain engagement throughout.
