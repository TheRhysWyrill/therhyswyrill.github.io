Special agent Shelia investigates abnormal weather phenomena in a near-future world, wielding ancient artefacts and teleporters against mysterious creatures. The premise establishes a sci-fi conspiracy involving primordial floods and supernatural technology, setting the stage for high-octane combat against relentless hostiles. From the opening moments, the visual fidelity dazzles with ray-traced lighting and detailed environments.

Combat demands split-second decisions between sword strikes, gunplay, and the grapple ability that pulls enemies into melee range. Sword and gun combos flow together with a hectic pace that rewards aggression, while the lightning power decimates clusters of enemies with devastating electrical discharge.

The shotgun delivers satisfying punch against tougher adversaries, with each blast creating visible impact against incoming hostiles. Multiple playthroughs unlock different costumes and abilities, though the core combat loop remains consistent across runs.

The interconnected level design reveals a sprawling metropolis with verticality and multiple pathways through each zone. Environmental destruction crumbles concrete barriers and shatters glass panes, creating dynamic combat arenas that shift mid-fight. Stealth sections add variety between firefights, though the system lacks the depth of the core combat mechanics.

Awkward control schemes hinder precise movement during platforming sequences, with button mapping creating confusion during heated encounters. A water reflection bug displays incorrect environmental data, breaking immersion during traversal sections. The final boss arena restricts movement to a confined space, limiting tactical options against aggressive adversaries.

Overall, the sword and gun hybrid combat delivers a visceral, fast-paced experience that rewards aggressive play, though technical glitches and control issues prevent the action from reaching its full potential.
