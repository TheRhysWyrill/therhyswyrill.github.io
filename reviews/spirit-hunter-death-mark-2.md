Returning protagonist Yoshiki investigates death notices at Kohara Academy, where students receive messages from a spirit called The Departed. The school setting echoes the first game's opening but expands into a deeper mystery involving stolen bodies and sister spirits. Hand-drawn visuals create genuine unease through bloodstained notices and horrifying spirit designs that maintain consistent tension across investigation sequences.

Side-scrolling investigation segments replace the previous visual novel format, with players navigating school corridors in a 2.5D perspective. The topic-based conversation system gives agency in how investigations proceed, avoiding purely linear dialogue trees. A coffee brewing mini-game serves as both save mechanism and character-building quirk that reinforces Yoshiki's personality.

Returning characters from the original Death Mark add continuity and warmth to the investigation, with the detective, idol, and other familiar faces providing emotional grounding. New spirit stories weave multiple grudges and tragic backstories into a layered narrative.

Health points add tension to spirit confrontations, where wrong choices during encounters can prove lethal. The chapter structure spans seven chapters plus a substantial bonus true ending chapter that rewards thorough investigation and careful decision-making.

The true ending chapter expands the narrative scope through layered spirit resolution mechanics, with character relationships and environmental clues determining the final outcome. Multiple spirit stories converge into a satisfying conclusion that justifies the investigation investment throughout the campaign.

Wrong decisions during investigations carry genuine consequences that maintain engagement throughout. Japanese name pronunciation difficulties create minor friction during character interactions, though this does not significantly impact the overall experience.

Overall, the hand-drawn visuals and atmosphere create a horror visual novel with genuine mechanical substance, while the true ending delivers an emotionally satisfying conclusion that elevates the entire experience.
