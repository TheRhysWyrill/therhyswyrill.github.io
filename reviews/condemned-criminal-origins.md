Urban decay and forensic investigation converge in this first-person survival horror where FBI agent Ethan Thomas navigates crime-ridden city environments. Dark corridors, abandoned buildings, and subway stations establish an oppressive atmosphere from the opening sequences. The premise sets up a detective-horror hybrid blending investigative deduction with visceral melee confrontation.

Forensic tools drive exploration through environmental interaction, with UV light revealing fingerprints, DNA analysis linking evidence across crime scenes, and developer fluid trails guiding progression. Solving a fingerprint match opens the next testimony sequence, building momentum between deductive steps. Evidence reconstruction mechanics provide satisfaction through logical deduction rather than waypoint navigation.

Improvised melee weapons dominate combat encounters, with fire axes, sledgehammers, paper cutters, and mannequin arms each offering distinct damage and speed characteristics. The taser upgrade transforms encounters by staggering enemies and creating tactical openings. Blocking remains frustrating throughout, with enemy fake-outs making timing unreliable and undermining the otherwise responsive combat system.

The fluorescent corridors and abandoned school environments build tension through environmental storytelling and escalating enemy encounters. Ceiling-crawling mutants and mannequin-disguised foes create peak horror moments. Darkness, ambient sound design, and tight corridors maintain constant unease without relying on cheap jump scares.

Forensic investigation mechanics sustain engagement through interconnected evidence chains and procedural reasoning. UV light, fingerprint analysis, and DNA profiling create tangible investigative flow. The progression system rewards careful observation, with each discovered clue linking to broader case connections.

The dark, oppressive atmosphere derives from urban decay, mannequin sequences, and escalating enemy types that shift from crackheads to ceiling-crawling mutants. Environmental detail reinforces the investigative narrative without relying on exposition.

Overall, the forensic investigation mechanics and weapon variety deliver a horror experience with genuine mechanical substance beneath the oppressive atmosphere. The blocking inconsistency and confusing control remapping during the final encounter undermine the otherwise methodical progression.
