If On A Winter's Night, Four Travelers is an exceptionally crafted adventure that captivates from the start with its beautiful pixel art. The visual style brings a remarkable amount of detail and atmosphere to every scene, drawing you deeply into the experience. The narrative structure is equally impressive, as each of the characters features a very interesting and distinct story explaining how they ultimately ended up at the mysterious masked ball. This variety in storytelling ensures that every perspective feels fresh and engaging. Furthermore, the outstanding music and ambient sound design work perfectly together to set the scene for each chapter, with a particular highlight being Samuel’s segment where the background noise of his arguing neighbours adds a brilliant layer of tension and realism to the setting. To top it all off, the game is completely free, which is incredible given the sheer quality of the presentation and writing.

Overall If On A Winter's Night, Four Travelers is a stunning and memorable narrative experience that punches well above its weight through evocative pixel artistry, masterful atmosphere, and an unbeatable price tag. By weaving together unique character vignettes with a perfectly tailored soundtrack, it delivers a deeply compelling psychological horror anthology that stands as an absolute must play.
