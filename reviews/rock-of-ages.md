A boulder-rolling tower defence lane race through art history defies classification, and that strangeness drives the experience. Each round splits into two halves. An overhead build phase places defences on green zones while the roll phase steers a boulder toward the enemy gate. Smashing the opposing door first decides every match.

Mammoths reshape the defensive meta once their value becomes clear. The shaggy units deal damage and physically shove the enemy boulder off ledges. Spamming them transforms narrow courses into slaughter alleys. Picture-in-picture camera views capture the wreckage from the opponent's perspective.

Boss fights drawn from paintings and statues punctuate the racing with anatomical weak-spot hunting. The dragon's chest demands targeted strikes between gate-smash sequences. Falls respawn the boulder nearby rather than restarting an attempt, keeping failure cheap.

The Porto stage represents the campaign's lowest point. This long, water-riddled course offers no good route, and losing track of the correct path costs entire attempts. Wind blowers compound the frustration, punting the boulder off ledges while seemingly leaving the AI untouched. TNT carpeting can likewise invalidate a run without counterplay.

Keys hide across courses with no explanation of their purpose. Their placement proves deliberately spiteful, and late levels suddenly demand them. Course layouts occasionally confuse during vertical or branching sections, particularly Florence's water-heavy design.

Cutscenes recreate canonical paintings with absurdist flair between stages. Post-campaign character unlocks extend the roster for repeat visits. The purchased fireball boulder gets destroyed frustratingly often, a recurring annoyance. A beneficial physics bug even earned affection rather than complaint.

Overall, a gleefully strange hybrid whose comedy and mechanical variety overwhelm its one horrid stage and occasional unfairness. The defend-then-roll loop stays tense throughout, and the art-history absurdity never wears thin across the full campaign.
