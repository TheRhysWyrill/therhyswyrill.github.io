A cyberpunk mega-city provides the backdrop for fast-paced FPS combat that blends Doom-style shooting with Titanfall-inspired wall-running. The movement system delivers immediate satisfaction, with wall-running and bullet time creating a fluid combat flow that sustains momentum across each encounter. Retro pixel art with modern lighting produces a visually striking aesthetic that enhances the cyberpunk atmosphere.

Weapon variety spans pistols, SMGs, shotguns, chain guns, and a rocket launcher, each serving distinct tactical purposes. Enemies drop health and ammo when eliminated through melee or headshots, rewarding aggressive playstyles. The chapter structure escalates difficulty gradually, with each section introducing new enemy configurations and environmental challenges.

The Spire section introduces platforming sequences that demand precise wall-running execution across narrow platforms, with some jumps requiring exact positioning. The aesthetic remains consistent throughout, with neon-lit corridors and industrial environments reinforcing the dystopian setting.

The chapter 2 boss fight stumbles with water-based mechanics that disrupt the otherwise tight movement system. Enemy patterns become obscured by environmental hazards, forcing repeated attempts that feel unfair rather than challenging.

The final boss encounters escalate enemy density to excessive levels, with spongy wall-mounted turrets absorbing disproportionate damage. The encounter design shifts from skill-based combat to attrition-based survival, undermining the movement system that defines the experience.

The soundtrack maintains energy throughout, providing electronic accompaniment that matches the cyberpunk visual identity. Value remains exceptional, with the complete experience available at a budget price point that belies the production quality.

Overall, the wall-running mechanics and retro pixel aesthetic deliver a visually striking FPS with satisfying combat flow. The frustrating final boss encounters prevent the experience from maintaining its momentum through the conclusion, creating a divisive ending to an otherwise tight shooter.
