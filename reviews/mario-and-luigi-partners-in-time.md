Bowser kidnaps Peach using a time machine, sending her to the past, and Mario and Luigi travel through time holes to rescue her whilst encountering baby versions of themselves. The premise establishes a humorous tone with four characters creating unique combat and exploration dynamics. Luigi's constant mistreatment and Stuffwell's verbose personality provide constant entertainment throughout the journey. The writing establishes genuine character-driven humour from the opening moments that persists throughout.

Turn-based combat with real-time action commands handles the four-character party system across varied encounters. Bros attacks changed from BP-based to consumable items, allowing spam but compensated by massive enemy health pools. Baby and adult character switching creates unique party compositions not seen in other RPGs. Skill tree and equipment systems provide meaningful progression across multiple areas including Holly Jolly Village.

Art direction presents detailed pixel art with expressive character animations that capture the series' humour. The time travel narrative allows revisiting locations with different party compositions, adding variety. Sound design features memorable compositions shifting between whimsical exploration themes and dramatic boss arrangements. The writing delivers consistently sharp dialogue with fourth-wall-breaking humour throughout.

Excessive front-loaded tutorials frustrate significantly with nine minutes of instruction before progress begins. The shift to item-based bros attacks proves prone to abuse, removing strategic depth. The DS top screen remains largely utilised as a simple map. Smash eggs require reading instructions to understand mechanics, creating confusion during combat.

Boss encounters escalate impressively, culminating in the Elder Princess Shrew's multi-phase fight with varied patterns. The instant saving system provides a major improvement over the previous entry.

Overall, the outstanding writing and characterisation deliver a brilliantly humorous RPG. Excessive tutorials and item-based bros attacks occasionally undermine the finest adventures.
