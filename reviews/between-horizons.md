Between Horizons drops Stella into the role of chief of security aboard the Zephyr, a ring-shaped generation ship carrying 1,300 people toward a distant star. Her investigation begins with her father's death and expands into conspiracies threatening the mission itself. The 2.5D pixel-art aesthetic renders the ship's corridors and chambers with a visual warmth that contrasts the tension of the unfolding mystery, while the ring-shaped layout creates a continuous loop that reinforces the claustrophobic setting.

Between investigations, environmental storytelling unfolds through scannable objects and audio logs scattered throughout the ship. Evidence collection rewards careful observation: scanning terminals, personal belongings, and structural details builds a case file that accumulates across chapters.

Timed dialogue choices inject urgency into conversations with suspects and crew members. Each exchange demands quick decisions about which questions to pursue, and the branching paths reshape the investigation's direction based on evidence already collected. This system transforms standard detective questioning into a pressure-cooking mechanic where hesitation carries consequences.

Environmental puzzles, embedded within the ship's systems, require scanning components and cross-referencing clues to unlock new areas. The ring design rewards traversal by revealing new sections as Stella circumnavigates the structure, with power systems and navigation consoles functioning as interactive elements.

Tutorial prompts, however, interrupt the investigative flow with excessive reminders that clutter the interface. The fast travel system, while functional, requires unnecessary menu navigation rather than direct point-to-point teleportation. Manual save functionality is absent, locking progression to automatic checkpoints.

Overall, the sci-fi detective premise and evidence-based investigation mechanics deliver an atmospheric mystery whose tension persists beyond its interface friction, creating a compelling investigative experience whose ring-shaped setting and timed dialogue system distinguish it within the genre.
