Twenty-four cups across the base game and DLC Booster Pass deliver an unrivalled content library with split-lap route designs. Anti-gravity sections establish unique racing dynamics from the opening moments where walls become surfaces. Every cup offers distinct track designs that reward memorisation and strategic item usage.

Grand Prix racing handles 150 CC as the primary mode with item-based combat requiring strategic timing and positioning. Character weight and vehicle customisation affect speed and acceleration across varied tracks. Auto-steer and smart steering toggles provide accessibility options that reduce difficulty. The Booster Pass DLC nearly doubles the track count with innovative designs.

Art direction presents vibrant, colourful track environments with detailed character models and kart designs. The soundtrack delivers memorable compositions shifting between upbeat racing themes and atmospheric arrangements. Sound design features satisfying audio feedback during item hits, drift boosts, and anti-gravity transitions.

The item balance heavily favours catch-up mechanics, creating situations where skill becomes secondary to RNG. Blue shells launch at least once per race, combining with red shells to punish leaders consistently.

The split-lap designs in DLC tracks offer innovative route variety where each lap traverses different parts of the track. Auto-switch to newly unlocked karts occasionally disrupts momentum between races.

The Booster Pass tracks expand visual variety while character selection adds personality through weight classes. Solo play proves enjoyable but limited without the chaos of multiplayer interactions.

Overall, the unrivalled content volume and outstanding track design create the definitive kart racer. Relentless blue shells and item-heavy balance occasionally undermine competitive integrity for front-runners.
