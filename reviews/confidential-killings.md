The year is1978, and Hollywood is reeling from a string of murders. Confidential Killings opens with a crime scene awaiting examination, inviting meticulous inspection of every object and character present. Clue-collection mechanics encourage careful observation, with each underlined detail feeding into a notebook that reconstructs events through player deduction. The retro pixel art aesthetic establishes the noir-inflected 1970s atmosphere, with muted colour palettes evoking crime film sensibilities.

Five interconnected cases build upon one another. Characters and plotlines carry over between investigations, rewarding attention to recurring details. From a roadside crash to a theatre murder to a mass hotel poisoning, the cases span months and expose a larger conspiracy threading through each event.

The notebook reconstruction mechanic transforms scattered clues into coherent narratives. Rather than following waypoints, evidence requires active synthesis: connecting who was present, what objects were involved, and how events unfolded. Solving a fingerprint match opens the next testimony sequence, creating momentum between deductive steps. Between crime scene examinations, witness testimonies add context that reshapes earlier assumptions about the case. The interconnected structure rewards careful tracking, as details surfaced in one investigation prove essential to cracking another.

The retro pixel art aesthetic captures the era's grit and glamour, with muted colour palettes and hand-drawn character portraits establishing the noir tone. Translation and grammar issues occasionally obscure meaning, with typos surfacing throughout descriptions. Some cases devolve into brute-forcing solutions when the logical connections lack sufficient clarity. The interconnected narrative sustains engagement across cases, carrying momentum from one investigation to the next.

Overall, Confidential Killings delivers a satisfying detective experience through its clue-collection and notebook reconstruction mechanics, though translation errors and opaque logic gates prevent the investigative flow from fully realising its potential. The interconnected cases and retro aesthetic create a distinctive noir atmosphere that sustains engagement despite these friction points.
