Confidential Killings delivers engaging deduction mechanics through a core gameplay system of collecting clues by clicking underlined words and reconstructing events in a notebook. This innovative design is paired with a compelling mystery and narrative that weaves a meaty story involving corporate espionage, hidden identities, and a dangerous drug empire. The overarching plot maintains excellent momentum across interconnected cases, providing an immersive experience for virtual detectives.

The game effectively establishes a serious, noir-inspired tone through an effective visual aesthetic and a minimalist yet cool musical score. Each investigation is structured with a well-paced progression that introduces increasingly complex elements, such as security camera footage, recorded tapes, and multiple victims to keep the gameplay fresh. Navigating these layers of evidence leads to deeply satisfying eureka moments, where correctly charting a complex chronology or exposing a hidden puppet master provides a strong sense of accomplishment.

However, the indie detective experience faces a few notable hurdles stemming from its technical polish and presentation. The game suffers from prominent translation and grammar issues, featuring numerous misspelled words and awkward sentence structures that occasionally make the dialogue and critical clues difficult to parse. This lack of textual clarity can result in significant brute-force potential, where solving certain cases devolves into trial-and-error clicking rather than pure logical deduction.

The gameplay loop also introduces some mechanical friction through repetitive character identification, forcing you to rediscover and re-assign names to the exact same recurring suspects in subsequent cases. This redundancy is compounded by vague user interface indicators that make it unclear why certain objects are flagged as clues or why specific sentence layouts are rejected by your notebook. Finally, the package feels slightly hollow due to a brief playtime that falls significantly shorter than advertised, even when pursuing full completion.

Overall Confidential Killings is a remarkably clever, atmospheric, and mechanically engaging noir detective game that shines brightest through its creative notebook reconstruction system, multi-layered corporate mystery, and rewarding deductions. While the investigation is frequently hindered by a poor English translation, repetitive naming chores, vague user interface feedback, and a short runtime, the excellent pacing and satisfying puzzle design ensure it remains a thoroughly compelling experience for fans of investigative logic games.
