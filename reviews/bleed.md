Rin dreams of becoming the world's greatest hero by defeating the current top champions in this fast-paced 2D action platformer with run-and-gun mechanics. The premise establishes a lighthearted, self-aware tone from the opening briefing, where character bios flesh out each opponent's backstory. Arcade-style energy drives every encounter from the start.

Slow-motion ability transforms combat into a stylish spectacle, allowing precise aiming and evasion during chaotic encounters. The mechanic rewards aggressive play by granting additional bullet time for successive hits, creating momentum that carries through each arena. Air dashes add verticality, enabling aerial combinations that blend shooting with acrobatic movement.

Weapon variety sustains engagement across the compact campaign, with pistols, shotgun, revolver, flamethrower, and RPG each suited to different combat scenarios. The flamethrower excels at close range but demands careful positioning, while the RPG provides crowd control against grouped enemies. A shop system between levels allows purchasing upgrades, though the shotgun proves frustratingly difficult to equip during heated moments.

Boss encounters showcase inventive mechanics that extend beyond simple shooting galleries. Platforming challenges, conveyor belt sequences, and multi-phase fights demand adapting to shifting patterns rather than relying on a single strategy. The forgiving checkpoint system encourages experimentation with different weapons and approaches without harsh punishment for failure.

The right-trigger jump mapping feels counterintuitive initially, requiring mental rewiring from standard platformer conventions. This unusual scheme becomes natural after a few encounters, though the adjustment period creates brief friction. Enemy dodging also demands heightened awareness, as certain projectiles punish inattentive movement.

Overall, the slow-motion combat and weapon variety deliver an arcade-style platformer that rewards aggressive play and creative loadout choices, despite an unconventional control scheme that demands patience during the opening hours.
