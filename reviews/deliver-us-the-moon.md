An unnamed astronaut departs for a silent lunar colony after Earth's wireless power grid collapses, carrying only a small companion drone and the hope of restoring connectivity. The premise establishes isolation and urgency from the opening moments, with abandoned facilities and scattered audio logs gradually reconstructing the disaster. From the first steps onto the moon's surface, atmosphere emerges through environmental detail rather than exposition.

Zero-gravity movement excels in vacuum sections, where weightless drifting through corridors and exterior hull breaches feels appropriately disorienting. Outside these sequences, the same floaty controls persist on solid ground, creating navigation that feels imprecise during platforming. Momentum carries beyond intended trajectories, particularly on narrow walkways where precision matters. Rover and monorail sections offer brief mechanical variety, though exploration beyond predetermined paths remains blocked.

Power conduit puzzles demand spatial reasoning across multi-room facilities. Environmental storytelling weaves through holographic recordings and abandoned personal quarters, building a believable near-future setting.

MPT alignment sequences introduce time-critical puzzle mechanics that test spatial awareness under pressure, rewarding careful observation of environmental clues. Each successful alignment triggers mechanical responses that unlock new areas. Corporate intrigue layers across each facility explored through holographic recordings.

Surface navigation suffers from the floaty movement model, where character momentum drifts beyond intended trajectories during precision platforming. UI elements appear dated with generic button designs and overly shiny collectible indicators that clash with the grounded aesthetic. Sprint functionality requires holding the left trigger, adding friction to traversal that accumulates across extended sequences. The companion drone's flashlight provides occasional navigation assistance, though its limited range offers little compensation for the imprecise controls.

Overall, the zero-gravity exploration and environmental puzzle design create a focused science fiction experience with real atmosphere, though the imprecise movement mechanics on solid ground prevent the environmental storytelling from fully sustaining its momentum.
