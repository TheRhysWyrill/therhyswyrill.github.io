The Fateless One awakens resurrected by the Well of Souls, a corpse without predetermined destiny in a world at war between immortal Fae and mortal races. The premise establishes genuine freedom to explore and shape the narrative across a sprawling fantasy landscape. Three distinct regions provide substantial variety, each with their own cultures, conflicts, and faction questlines. The re-release delivers polished visuals and substantial content across the base game and three DLCs.

Real-time combat handles multiple weapon types with fluid juggling mechanics that reward mastery. The classless progression system mixes Might, Finesse, and Sorcery with destiny cards that unlock specialisations as skills are invested. Enemy variety spans Tuatha, Bolgans, and Niskaru, keeping encounters fresh. The scaling system ensures challenges remain relevant, though it occasionally creates artificial difficulty spikes.

Art direction presents a vibrant fantasy world with visual style by Todd McFarlane, featuring distinct environments. The remastered textures and lighting enhance the original's presentation without losing its character. Sound design features atmospheric music that shifts between combat intensity and exploration calm. Voice acting adds personality to faction interactions throughout the world.

Inventory management proves cumbersome with no easy equipment swapping or quick comparison tools. Some dungeon layouts feel repetitive with excessive enemies obscuring progression. The Fatesworn DLC's armour collection quest demands lengthy dungeons followed by large rifts.

The shakram weapons deliver a standout experience with stylish juggling capabilities. Multiple faction questlines provide narrative variety throughout the expansive world.

Overall, this massively content-rich action RPG rewards exploration and combat mastery. Cumbersome inventory management and tedious dungeon layouts occasionally undermine the otherwise fluid experience.
