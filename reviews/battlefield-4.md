Battlefield 4 drops a squad of soldiers into a near-future conflict spanning Shanghai, the South China Sea, and the Suez Canal. The opening submarine disaster sets a chaotic tone, and the visual smoothness immediately registers: movement, destruction, and particle effects flow with a fluidity that distinguishes it from its predecessor. From the start, environmental destruction dominates the impression, with buildings collapsing and walls shattering under sustained fire.

Combat revolves around standard military FPS mechanics: squad commands, gadget loadouts, and class-based weapon selection. The shotgun delivers particular impact, each blast carrying weight and auditory punch that makes close-quarters encounters satisfying. Vehicle sections, including a boat chase and a dam explosion, punctuate the infantry combat with cinematic variety.

The squad engage system attempts tactical depth by allowing enemies to be tagged for AI teammates to target. In practice, the system requires holding a button to tag but pressing the same button triggers shooting, creating accidental discharges during tense moments. AI companions prove unreliable, offering minimal support during firefights and failing to capitalise on tagged targets.

Lens flares represent the campaign's most persistent problem, obscuring gameplay with bright flashes that cannot be toggled off. Enemies absorb excessive damage, requiring extended bursts to dispatch, which undermines the weapons' apparent power. The menu system compounds these issues with a cluttered interface that obscures basic navigation.

Vehicle sequences maintain momentum between infantry sections, and Irish emerges as a character whose loyalty carries emotional weight through the squad's journey. The detection meter, an odd addition to a military shooter, feels disconnected from the surrounding combat systems.

Overall, the environmental destruction and smooth visual delivery create a military shooter with mechanical substance, even if lens flares and bullet-spongy enemies interrupt the otherwise competent campaign flow.
