Yaga searches for her missing twin sister Zora through a Slavic folklore-inspired forest, where classic fairy tales receive dark reimaginings across four seasons. The premise blends familiar tales like Sleeping Beauty, Snow White, and Goldilocks with Slavic myth. Childhood stories carry sinister undertones revealed through collectible tomes scattered throughout the world.

The morality system shapes the experience from the opening hours, tracking good and evil deeds that determine which NPCs appear and which quest options become available. This branching approach means revisiting areas under different moral alignments, discovering new encounters tied to previous choices. The seasonal progression gates content naturally, with each phase introducing distinct visual palettes and tonal shifts.

Combat centres on bow-based archery against forest creatures and corrupted enemies, with arrow types including sticky, explosive, and standard variants. Sneaking mechanics add stealth options, though the controls feel imprecise during movement, especially when crouching or strafing around obstacles. Small, fast-moving enemies prove difficult to hit reliably, creating frustration during encounters that demand quick reflexes.

The 2D platforming segments break up the exploration with puzzle-platformer sections that carry unique visual treatments. These moments provide welcome variety, shifting the perspective and mechanics while maintaining the atmospheric fairy tale tone. Mr. Lava, a recurring character, adds personality to the journey with memorable interactions scattered across the forest.

Environmental storytelling unfolds through the seasonal cycle, with spring's vibrant forests giving way to autumn's decay and winter's stark beauty. The blink idol fast-travel system connects distant areas, reducing tedious backtracking across the expansive forest. Navigation between quest markers sometimes proves unclear, with no clear direction for the next objective.

Overall, the Slavic folklore setting and morality-driven narrative deliver a fairy tale adventure with visual charm and branching consequences, though awkward controls and imprecise combat prevent the bow-focused mechanics from reaching their full potential.
