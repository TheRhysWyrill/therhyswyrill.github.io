TR-49 introduces a highly unique and fascinating concept that beautifully captures the deductive spirit of games like Return of the Obra Dinn. Instead of piecing together the fates of a lost crew, your objective revolves around uncovering specific book titles based on clues gathered during a quest for Endpeace. The game handles this information with a wonderful sense of subtlety, giving you just enough direction to get started while leaving plenty of distinct avenues to explore. This hands-off design naturally encourages careful observation and note-taking, as the details are subtle enough that you might occasionally forget a specific author or publication year and need to double-check past clues. The game even features a highly creative segment that plays out exactly like a choose your own adventure book, though it is a bit unfortunate that this memorable section does not last very long.

Complementing the central investigative mechanics is a narrative that cleverly carries on in the background, keeping you engaged with the occasional required player input. This unfolding story is brought to life by excellent voice acting, particularly through the well-delivered and natural interactions between the characters Abbi and Liam. However, the experience is slightly hampered when playing with a controller, as the layout does not feel optimized. This leads to a decent chunk of unintentional misinputs during gameplay, which can become a minor annoyance as you navigate the menus and text. Fortunately, these control quirks are never entirely detrimental to your overall progress, remaining a small blemish on an otherwise smooth historical puzzle loop.

Overall, TR-49 is a deeply engaging and intelligent investigative game that stands out through its brilliant book-tracking premise, subtle world-building, and excellent voice work. While the brief nature of its choose your own adventure sequence and some finicky controller inputs can occasionally cause minor frustrations, the satisfying loop of deduction and research makes it a must-play for fans of atmospheric mystery games.
