A mystery puzzle game unfolds through a machine that eats books and reveals hidden messages, with Abby communicating from inside whilst codes input fragmented texts. The opening moments establish cerebral atmosphere through garbled text that gradually clears. From the start, the code-breaking mechanics reward careful observation and note-taking.

The puzzle design demands reading comprehension as letter-number codes decode fragmented entries. Titles and context clues emerge through careful examination of journal entries and academic papers. These mechanics create satisfying progression as text clarity improves with each discovery.

Controller controls prove awkward for typing sequences, requiring keyboard and mouse for optimal experience. The wealth of information across 50 sources creates easy distraction from primary objectives. These design choices occasionally disrupt focus during complex deduction sequences.

The multiple endings provide meaningful choice based on which books are destroyed or preserved. The choose-your-own-adventure segments add narrative variety through distinct story paths. These branching conclusions reward engagement with the broader mystery beyond immediate puzzle solutions.

No title screen or save reset creates frustration when attempting fresh starts. Abby's dialogue occasionally interrupts the contemplative pace with unnecessary commentary. These minor issues contrast with the otherwise polished narrative delivery.

The machine-as-narrator concept distinguishes the experience through visible text clearing that provides tangible feedback. Finding Endpiece creates emotional peak through clever deduction and persistent investigation. These moments demonstrate the puzzle design's ability to reward thorough engagement.

Overall, the code-breaking mechanics and machine-eating-books concept deliver an engaging puzzle experience with satisfying narrative discovery, though controller controls and information overload occasionally interrupt the otherwise cerebral deduction flow.
