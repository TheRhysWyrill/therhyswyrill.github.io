{%- comment -%} Thumbnail path for a review cover, worked out in exactly one place. The archive paints its cards from two renderers — _includes/review-card.html for the first page, the client renderer in pages/reviews.html for the rest — and both have to point at the same files, so the build-time cards and the card index (pages/reviews-cards.json) both include this and read `review_thumb` back out of the caller's scope (Liquid includes share scope). `review_thumb` is empty when the cover is not a local review image (nothing else has a generated thumbnail), and callers then emit no srcset at all rather than a broken one. Usage: {% include review-thumb.html image=card.image %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _thumb_image = include.image | default: "" -%} {%- assign review_thumb = "" -%} {%- if _thumb_image contains "/assets/img/reviews/" and _thumb_image contains ".webp" -%} {%- assign _thumb_parts = _thumb_image | split: "/" -%} {%- assign _thumb_file = _thumb_parts | last -%} {%- assign _thumb_head = _thumb_parts | pop | join: "/" -%} {%- assign review_thumb = _thumb_head | append: "/thumbs/" | append: _thumb_file -%} {%- endif -%}
